What's the deal...

…with live music?

 Now i understand the appeal of seeing a band you know in concert or one which involves audience participation like an open mic night or a band that takes requests..

 But what i’m talking about is when you go to a bar/pub and it boasts on a sign that it has live music, you go inside and its just a random band blaring out music, not covers, but usually inane warbling you can’t decipher. Firstly, it’s always too loud and you can’t hear anything, so having any form of conversation is out of the question and secondly i find it awkward that they expect applause after every number they perform. But the worst types of these are ‘jam nights’ where they play whatever they want and try to make it work. I suppose you might discover a new band you liked, but surely that can be achieved at an open mic night, so other people have a chance to show off or you come to see your friends play?

 Why they would do this instead of putting in a jukebox so patrons can choose their own music, i’ll never know. Hell, i’d even settle for them just to turn it down and not make such a huge fucking deal about the music and focus everything in the room they possibly can on the tiny hand-built stage in the corner.

tl;dr -shitty bands are shitty no matter how loud. 

(never usually vent like this so excuse me)
