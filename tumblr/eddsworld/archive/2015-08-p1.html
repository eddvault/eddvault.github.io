Wanna Draw a Comic?

Hey Eddheads, Tom here!

A couple months ago I rebooted the Eddsworld comics and I’ve been working with you guys to illustrate them (hence the different art styles). I’ve been getting a lot of questions like “can I draw one?” and it’s so nice to see so many of you wanting to get involved. So, I thought I’d open up the gates and tell you how!

If you’d like to draw an Eddsworld comic then go ahead and… Draw one! Doesn’t have to be funny or anything, I’m just looking for folk who get the style/characters and wanna be a part of the show. You can post your comic on Twitter, Tumblr or DeviantArt and as long as you put “eddsworld” in the tags you can be sure I’ll find it. The final thing I’d like you to do is include your email address on the comic so I know how to contact you directly. Apologies in advance if I don’t reach out to you - we only need one comic a week and if like 100 people draw comics then… Well… You get the idea…

Now, I’m funding this project myself which is why I can’t afford to pay more than £15/$25 per comic (or whatever that amounts to where you’re from) so please know that before offering your services - I don’t want anyone feeling used or anything.

Thanks gang! I look forward to seeing what you cook up :D

- Tom
