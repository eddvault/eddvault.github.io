CALLING ALL COMPOSERS

eddsworld:

Hey Eddheads! Tom here. So it’s my last 2 weeks with Eddsworld before handing it back over to the Gould family and I’m doing my best to leave the show with a bang. Today I’m going to be releasing 4 old Eddisodes onto the Eddsworld Legacy channel. These include 2 eddisodes which were removed due to copyright strikes (Zombeh Attack and Halloween 2007) and 2 which never made it to YouTube at all (Xmas 2005, and Behind the Scenes of Eddsworld).

Now! I’ve removed all the copyrighted music from these eddisodes so they don’t get taken down and - hopefully - that’s where you come in! I actively encourage any aspiring musicians to download the eddisodes and add your own music. If it sounds great I’ll reach out, grab your tunes, and reupload a 100% final version! You’ll be credited too, of course! I’d like to be able to offer you money for your music but this is just a bonus project I’m throwing together out of love for the show. I’m hoping others will feel the same.

Thank you and standby for updates!

- Tom

Here they are! 4 old-school eddisodes (1, 2, 3, 4) in desperate need of music! Download ‘em, write some music, then send your score over to ewsoundtrack@gmail.com before the 10th of March! We’ll pick our favourites, edit them into the episodes, and credit you fully! Thanks :D
