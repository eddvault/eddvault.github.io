Eddsworld Q&A

Hey Eddheads! Tom here. We get a lot of questions about the show so I thought I’d answer a few of your most commonly asked questions now!

1) WHERE ARE THE NEW EDDISODES!? - They’re coming! Mirror Mirror is taking a while because Sandra is juggling a full-time job and only has time to animate in the evenings; Saloonatics is getting a new animator so obviously that’s going to take a while longer - it’s 10 minutes long; The End is even longer  and won’t be coming out until after everything else is done. Standby and when we have updates we’ll post ‘em (please don’t pester the animators).

2) WHAT ABOUT THE DOCUMENTARY?! - That’s coming too! The documentary was originally going to be a 10 minute behind-the-scenes just for the Legacy donators but since we’ve kept you waiting so long we’ve turned it into a 40 minute long monster all about the past and present of the show. It’s really cool. We’ll release that to the donators when it’s done and onto the channel for everyone a little while later.

3) WHAT HAPPENS AFTER ‘THE END’? - Whatever happens, I’ll be stepping down as show-runner. I wrote a little more about that [here].

4) ARE YOU PLANNING ON MAKING AN EDDSWORLD DVD? - I’ll be honest with you, I’m not sure if that will ever happen. To make a DVD possible we’d essentially need to remake every episode from before 2012 (new sound design, music, etc) and that’d take hundreds of hours. It was a stupidly ambitious promise I made and I apologise.

5) WHERE’S THE CHARITY MONEY?! - So far we’ve donated over £40,000 to charity and by the time Mirror Mirror comes out we’ll have raised that amount to over £65,000! All of that is thanks to you.

6) IS TORD COMING BACK? - The voice actor? No. The character? I couldn’t possibly say.

7) CAN WE BUY ANY NEW MERCHANDISE? - It’s hard to say because we have to buy/order merchandise in bulk which means guaranteeing that hundreds/thousands of people are gonna be interested. It’s just a little too risky.

8) WILL THE WEBSITE BE COMING BACK? - Again, probably not. We post updates on Twitter, blog posts on Tumblr, videos on YouTube and comics everywhere. Aside from making a forum for the “fandom” there’s not much need for a dedicated site. Tumblr is a pretty lively place as far as the Eddsworld fandom goes though!

9) WHAT DO I DO IF I HAVE MORE QUESTIONS, TOM?! - You ask ‘em to me on Twitter or Tumblr and I’ll do my best to answer the ones that aren’t extremely stupid!

- Tom
