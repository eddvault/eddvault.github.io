Top five tips for people trying to draw cartoons for the first time? I only doodle and want to get better at it.

- Finish all your sketches. Yes even those you hate. Make them into something.
- Trace newspaper pictures. Sounds silly, but it really helps you figure out faces.
- Never throw away old art. Yes, that means you. You know who you are.
- Have a sense of humour about drawing. Don’t take it so seriously, if it messes up have a laugh with it. I cannot stress this enough.
- Mirrors help a lot to make stupid faces.
